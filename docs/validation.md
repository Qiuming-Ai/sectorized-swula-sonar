# Packaging validation

Local validation used Python 3.14.7, NumPy 2.4.6, SciPy 1.17.1, Matplotlib 3.10.9, pandas 3.0.5 and PyMuPDF 1.28.0. These are the versions used to check the package, not a claim about all historical runs.

- The Python design entry point rendered Figures 2, 3, 4(a), 4(b), 7 and 8. MATLAB R2025b exercised the archived A1/A19 configuration and common helper functions; the full historical MATLAB runs were not repeated.
- The figure scripts regenerated Figures 5, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 14. Figure 10/11 outputs were verified against the archived rendering on this machine. Figure 14 rechecked mean and CI against the trial table, including the negative CDM angular-profile correlation.
- With the companion asset extracted, Figure 13 was redrawn from the saved grids. The PNG matched the archived rendering byte for byte, and the rendered PDF matched the original manuscript figure pixel for pixel on this machine. The shipwreck numerical entry point loaded the archived arrival catalogue in its original row order; the full 30-realization numerical experiment was not repeated. The two README PNGs are byte-identical copies of the archived Figure 13 and 15 renderings.
- The v10 MATLAB R2025b checks covered 57 revision sources, 13 configurations, waveform generation and A8 input preflight, plus the Figure 2 prototype. A1 completed in quick mode. The A9 audit, A11 archived-arrival scene construction and A16/small-grid reconstruction checks passed. The STL MEX build reached the CUDA compiler but was rejected by the local host-compiler compatibility check; no prebuilt MEX binary is distributed. The no-CUDA A11 archive path was checked independently.
- The desktop MATLAB check loaded the three exact HDF5 inputs and reconstructed one ping per method. The historical 100-row timing log reproduced the recorded Dense/CDM/FDM means of 265.61/60.11/31.27 ms. A new 100-ping timing campaign was not run.
- The numerical scene gates passed all four CZT/direct or grid/core checks. The dense scene reran using the portable reflectivity NPZ; all 720 trial rows and 24 summary rows matched the archived values within numerical tolerance. The full 30-m point synthesis was not repeated during packaging; the exact figure inputs were re-rendered.
- The NMS script reran all 6,480 method/trial/setting records from the archived full point-noise bank. Its primary-detector comparison passed all 720 checks; the 216 summary rows agreed with the archived table.
- The broadband candidate coverage search and final grid audit reran, and the finite-template window control matched the archived 20 metrics rows. An independent physical-element sum passed all 18 checks (maximum relative complex L2 difference below `8e-16`). Full ambiguity, all-bank two-way, progressive-density and DAS/OMP simulation sweeps were not repeated for packaging.
- The Jetson audit recomputed all 1,800 timing records, nine power integrals and 85,439 completed workload blocks from the archived logs. The records were not reacquired, and no new Jetson build is claimed. See the [Jetson validation and usage notes](../code/jetson/README.md) for build, fixture and replay boundaries.

The package checksum and data-contract command is `python scripts/check_release.py`. It checks the staged files and selected row counts, pairings, means and confidence intervals. It does not certify redistribution rights or replace a simulation or hardware rerun.
